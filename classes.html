Classes

All Equipment Provided & Safety First: Every group class includes recurve or compound bows, arrows, finger tabs, armguards, and targets at no extra cost. Every session begins with range safety rules and whistle command checks led by USA Archery-certified instructors.

A101 Basic Archery

A101 is a basic archery course that focuses on beginning archery safety, skills, and drills. Participants learn about archery form, range rules, and etiquette. Form training and fun games are introduced in this class. All equipment will be provided with exception of safety kit.

  • Schedule: Mondays and Wednesdays, 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Cost: $120 / month (plus tax)
  • Prerequisites: None — Interest in learning about the sport of archery. Minimum age 8 years old (or 7 years old with pre-evaluation).
Enroll / Ask

A102 Intermediate Archery

A102 is an intermediate course that focuses on intermediate archery safety, form, and also introduces new equipment styles of shooting Recurve and Compound bows. Fun games and form reinforcement techniques are emphasized in this class. All equipment will be provided with exception of safety kit.

  • Schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Cost: $120 / month (plus tax)
  • Prerequisites: A101 or recommendation from private lesson instructor. Minimum age 7 years old.
Enroll / Ask

A103 Advanced Archery

After taking the A101, A102 classes, students are eligible to participate in the A103 class. This class focuses on a continued education towards tournament styles of shooting used by many archery tournament associations from around the world. Along with the tournament lesson, participants receive continued knowledge around their shooting form and advice on personal equipment selections. All equipment will be provided with exception of safety kit.

  • Schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  • Cost: $120 / month (plus tax)
  • Prerequisites: Completion of A101 and A102 or equivalent. Minimum age 7 years old.
Enroll / Ask
