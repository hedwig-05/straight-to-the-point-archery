Our Classes

Structured step-by-step group courses from beginner fundamentals to competitive tournament mastery. All bows, arrows, and safety gear are included.

A101 Basic

Designed for students with no prior experience. Learn range safety, stance, bow handling, and shooting technique at 10 yards. All equipment provided.

Learn More

A102 Intermediate

Builds on A101 fundamentals with form refinement, sight adjustments, recurve & compound styles, and tournament scoring protocols.

Learn More

A103 Advanced

High-performance coaching focused on competitive biomechanics, custom equipment tuning, mental preparation, and USA Archery tournament readiness.

Learn More

Why Straight to the Point

Parents, beginners, and serious athletes trust our center for uncompromised safety and professional coaching.

Certified Instructors

Every coach holds USA Archery National Training System (NTS) certification along with current SafeSport, First Aid, and CPR credentials.

Great Facilities

28 indoor shooting lanes (14 at 10 yards and 14 at 20 yards), private coaching bays, and full beginner-to-advanced rental equipment included.

Safety First

Strict whistle range commands, mandatory first-day safety orientations, fitted armguards, and supervised shooting lines keep every archer safe.
