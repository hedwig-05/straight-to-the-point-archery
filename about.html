About Us

Learn about our community mission, tour our 28-lane indoor range and equipment facilities, and read our nondiscriminatory policy.

Mission and History

Straight to the Point Archery is a non-profit archery education center founded in 2005 by Giap Nguyen. Our state-of-the-art educational and training facility incorporates detailed technical video analysis combined with generations of coaching experience from certified instructors.

Giap Nguyen started Straight to the Point Archery in 2005. He's an avid and accomplished archer himself, having competed with the United States Archery Team in the 2000 Summer Olympics. He has three children, and started Straight to the Point in part because he was not satisfied with the archery eduction options available to them in Tacoma.

We have strong community connections with the USA Archery (USAA), Washington State Archery Association (WSAA), Washington State Bow Hunters (WSB), National Field Archery Association (NFAA), local Boy and Girl Scouts, YMCA, and corporate team-building groups.

Our Facilities

Our indoor training center features 28 shooting lanes, two private coaching rooms, and full rental equipment for archers and groups of all sizes.

Range

28 shooting lanes (14 at 10-yard and 14 at 20-yard distances) in a climate-controlled environment.

Equipment

Complete fleet of beginner, youth, and adult recurve and compound bows included for all students.

Lobby

Comfortable parent viewing area, check-in counter, and community lounge for families and groups.

Targets

Olympic and NFAA regulation target butts with heavy-duty safety backstops and clear lane markers.

Gear Room

Custom bow tuning bench, arrow maintenance station, fitted armguards, and finger tabs.

Outdoor

Seasonal outdoor practice lawn for corporate team-building events, scout troops, and workshops.

Nondiscriminatory Policy

Next Step Archery / Straight to the Point Archery Nondiscriminatory Policy:

Next Step Archery admits students of any race, gender presentation, sexuality, religion, national origin or ethnic origin to all rights, privileges, programs and activities available to students at the school. We do not discriminate in administration of our educational policies, admission policies and other school administered programs. All students are welcome and celebrated in our community.
